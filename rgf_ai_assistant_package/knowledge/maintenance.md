# Knowledge Base Maintenance

The knowledge base is the assistant’s approved business source. Update it whenever RGF changes a public service, capability, industry, engagement statement, policy, contact route, or approved FAQ.

## Publishing workflow

1. A business owner edits the relevant Markdown source.
2. A second reviewer confirms accuracy, public suitability, and privacy.
3. Assign a version and effective date.
4. Replace the previous file in the vector store; do not retain conflicting active versions.
5. Run the regression and refusal tests.
6. Publish only after approval.

## Rules

- Prices, availability, delivery dates, partner facts, case studies, customer names, certifications, and metrics require explicit written approval.
- Remove outdated claims; do not rely on a newer file to override a contradictory older file.
- Keep policy files separate from business facts.
- Review quarterly and after every material website or service change.
