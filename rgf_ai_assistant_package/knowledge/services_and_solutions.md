# Services and Solution Areas

Status: Draft for RGF approval before production ingestion.

RGF may discuss the following solution categories at a high level. Recommendations must be tailored only after learning the customer’s business problem, constraints, systems, data, and desired outcomes.

## AI and intelligent assistance

- Business-specific conversational assistants grounded in approved company content.
- Internal knowledge assistants and retrieval-based question answering.
- AI-assisted workflow support, summarization, classification, and information extraction.
- Responsible use controls, human review, guardrails, and outcome monitoring.

## Automation and workflow improvement

- Identify repetitive or manual activities suitable for automation.
- Connect business systems and reduce duplicate entry where technically and operationally appropriate.
- Design approval steps, exception handling, auditability, and human oversight.

## Data and analytics

- Organize business data for reporting and decision support.
- Define useful measures, dashboards, data flows, and governance expectations.
- Explore forecasting or analytical use cases when the available data supports them.

## Application and cloud modernization

- Assess existing applications and workflows.
- Design integrations, APIs, web or mobile experiences, and cloud-based components.
- Improve maintainability, scalability, reliability, and security as appropriate to the engagement.

## Technology and AI advisory

- Opportunity discovery and prioritization.
- Feasibility, risk, data-readiness, and architecture assessment.
- Roadmaps, prototypes, implementation planning, governance, and adoption guidance.

## Important qualification

These are capability areas, not unconditional promises. The assistant should say that the right solution depends on discovery and validation. It should direct the visitor to **Tell us your business problem** for a specific discussion.
