# Ask RGF — Approved FAQ Draft

Status: Draft for RGF approval before production ingestion.

## What does RGF Intelligence Solutions do?

RGF helps organizations explore and apply AI, automation, data, and modern technology to practical business problems. The work begins with the desired business outcome and considers feasibility, risk, existing systems, available data, user needs, and how value will be measured.

## What solutions do you provide?

RGF’s solution areas include business-specific AI assistants, knowledge retrieval, workflow automation, data and analytics, system integration, application and cloud modernization, and technology or AI advisory. The appropriate solution depends on discovery and validation.

## How can you help my company?

RGF can begin by understanding your current process, pain points, goals, systems, data, constraints, and users. From there, RGF can help identify feasible opportunities and define an approach with clear success measures and risks.

## What if the solution does not bring value?

RGF recommends agreeing on measurable outcomes and validating assumptions before broad scaling. If evidence does not support the expected benefit, the approach can be adjusted, the use case can be changed, or further investment can be stopped.

## How can we engage RGF?

Use **Tell us your business problem** on the website. Share the business challenge and desired outcome without including confidential, personal, or sensitive information in the chat.

## Can RGF integrate with our current systems?

RGF can explore integrations with existing systems when suitable APIs, data access, permissions, security requirements, and technical constraints are understood. Compatibility cannot be confirmed without discovery.

## Can you provide a price or delivery date now?

Pricing and timelines depend on scope, complexity, integrations, data readiness, security and compliance needs, and delivery expectations. The assistant does not quote prices or commit to delivery dates. Use **Tell us your business problem** to begin a specific discussion.

## Who owns RGF, and how many partners or employees does it have?

I can assist with information about RGF Intelligence Solutions’ services, solutions, industries, and engagement process. Information about ownership, partners, personnel, and internal company operations is not available through this assistant.
