# Industries and Example Use Cases

Status: Draft for RGF approval before production ingestion.

RGF can discuss cross-industry business problems and may use examples to make possibilities easier to understand. Examples are illustrative and must not be presented as completed client work.

## Automotive, service, and fleet operations

- Product or parts information assistance connected to approved catalogs and business systems.
- Service workflow support, order assistance, inventory visibility, and customer inquiry handling.
- Operational reporting and exception identification.

## Manufacturing and distribution

- Workflow automation, document processing, quality information access, and operational analytics.
- Inventory, order, and supply-process visibility where supported by connected systems.

## Retail and commerce

- Product knowledge assistance, staff support, customer-service automation, and operational reporting.
- Integration with existing POS, inventory, catalog, or order systems through supported APIs or data feeds.

## Financial and insurance operations

- Document intake, classification, knowledge access, workflow support, and analytics with appropriate privacy, compliance, and human review.

## Hospitality and food operations

- Customer inquiry support, internal knowledge access, operational workflows, and reporting.

## Life sciences and other regulated environments

- Controlled knowledge access, workflow support, and data solutions subject to the organization’s legal, privacy, security, validation, and regulatory requirements.

## Safe phrasing

Say: `A possible use case is…` or `RGF can explore whether…`.

Do not say: `RGF has delivered this for [client]` unless that case study is added to an approved source.
