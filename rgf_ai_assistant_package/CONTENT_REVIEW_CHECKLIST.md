# Content Review Checklist

Before uploading the package to the production knowledge base, an authorized RGF reviewer should confirm:

- [ ] Company description and tagline are approved.
- [ ] Every listed capability is currently offered or suitable for public discussion.
- [ ] Industry examples are acceptable and clearly presented as examples, not client claims.
- [ ] No customer, partner, owner, employee, private contact, or personal details are included.
- [ ] No unapproved prices, timelines, guarantees, certifications, metrics, or case studies are included.
- [ ] The engagement and value language matches how RGF wants to work with customers.
- [ ] `/contact` is the correct stable route for **Tell us your business problem**.
- [ ] Privacy, retention, logging, and lead-capture behavior are approved.
- [ ] Restricted and off-topic responses match RGF policy.
- [ ] All critical acceptance tests pass in production.
